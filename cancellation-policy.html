Cancellation Policy

We understand that travel plans can change. This policy explains how cancellation requests, booking changes and refunds are handled through Roamora Travels.

Last Updated: October 2026

At Roamora Travels, we understand that unexpected circumstances may require you to change or cancel your travel plans. Cancellation charges and refund eligibility may vary depending on the services included in your booking, the date of cancellation and the terms of the relevant travel service providers.

1. General Cancellation Policy

A traveller wishing to cancel a confirmed booking should inform Roamora Travels as soon as reasonably possible.

Cancellation requests will be processed according to the conditions applicable to the confirmed booking and the policies of hotels, transport providers, activity operators and other suppliers involved in the itinerary.

Cancellation charges may increase as the scheduled travel date approaches because travel services are often reserved and paid for in advance.

2. How to Request a Cancellation

Cancellation requests should be submitted through an official Roamora Travels communication channel and should include enough information for us to identify the relevant booking.

A cancellation request may require:

  • Name of the primary traveller
  • Booking or enquiry reference, where available
  • Travel destination
  • Scheduled travel dates
  • Contact information used for the booking
  • A clear request to cancel the relevant travel arrangement

A cancellation will be treated as received when Roamora Travels receives and acknowledges the cancellation request through an appropriate communication channel.

3. Cancellation Charges

Cancellation charges are not necessarily the same for every booking. The applicable amount may depend on the specific services included in your itinerary.

Cancellation charges may include:

  • Hotel or accommodation cancellation charges
  • Transport cancellation charges
  • Airline, railway or other ticket cancellation charges
  • Activity or sightseeing cancellation charges
  • Permit or entry-related charges
  • Supplier or service-provider penalties
  • Non-refundable amounts already committed for the booking

Important: The cancellation terms communicated with your confirmed quotation or booking will apply to that particular journey where they differ from general information provided on this page.

4. Non-Refundable Travel Services

Certain travel services may become partially or completely non-refundable once they have been booked or confirmed.

These may include, depending on the booking:

  • Non-refundable hotel reservations
  • Promotional or special-rate accommodation
  • Flight or transportation tickets
  • Special activities and experiences
  • Entry tickets and permits
  • Advance supplier payments
  • Services booked for specific dates or travellers

Where a supplier classifies a service as non-refundable, Roamora Travels may be unable to recover or refund that amount following cancellation.

5. Refund Eligibility

Where a booking is eligible for a refund, the refundable amount will generally be determined after deducting applicable cancellation charges, non-refundable supplier costs and other amounts already committed for the travel arrangement.

Refund eligibility will therefore depend on the individual booking and cannot be guaranteed solely because a cancellation request has been submitted.

6. Refund Processing

Eligible refunds will be processed after the applicable cancellation conditions have been reviewed and, where necessary, after refundable amounts have been confirmed or received from the relevant travel service providers.

Processing times may vary depending on the payment method, banking system and third-party suppliers involved in the booking.

Roamora Travels will make reasonable efforts to process eligible refunds without unnecessary delay after the refundable amount has been established.

7. Changes Instead of Cancellation

If your travel plans change, you may request a modification instead of cancelling the entire journey.

Changes may include:

  • Travel dates
  • Hotel or accommodation category
  • Destinations or itinerary route
  • Transportation arrangements
  • Activities or sightseeing
  • Number of travellers

Requested changes are subject to availability and may result in revised prices, supplier charges or other additional costs.

8. Rescheduling & Date Changes

Where possible, Roamora Travels may assist travellers who wish to reschedule their journey rather than cancel it.

Rescheduling is subject to availability, seasonal pricing and the policies of the travel providers included in the booking. Previously confirmed rates cannot always be carried forward to new travel dates.

9. No-Show

A traveller may be considered a no-show when they fail to use a confirmed travel service without cancelling or informing Roamora Travels within an appropriate period.

No-show bookings may be treated as fully non-refundable, depending on the conditions of the hotel, transport provider, activity operator or other supplier involved.

10. Unused Travel Services

Refunds are generally not guaranteed for travel services that have been confirmed but voluntarily left unused after the journey has started.

This may include unused hotel nights, sightseeing, transportation, meals, activities or other services included in the confirmed itinerary.

Any refund for an unused service will depend on the applicable supplier's terms and whether the amount can reasonably be recovered.

11. Early Departure or Trip Interruption

If a traveller voluntarily shortens or interrupts a journey after travel has begun, unused portions of the itinerary may not be refundable.

Where possible, Roamora Travels may assist in requesting refunds or alternative arrangements from the relevant service providers, but recovery of such amounts cannot be guaranteed.

12. Cancellation by a Travel Service Provider

Hotels, airlines, transport operators, activity providers and other independent suppliers may occasionally cancel, modify or reschedule their services.

Where this occurs, Roamora Travels will make reasonable efforts to assist with available alternatives, rescheduling or applicable supplier refunds.

Any refund relating to a third-party service may remain subject to the supplier's applicable terms and refund process.

13. Cancellation by Roamora Travels

In unusual circumstances, Roamora Travels may need to cancel or materially alter travel arrangements when providing the original itinerary becomes impossible, unsafe or impractical.

Where appropriate, we will make reasonable efforts to offer alternative arrangements, revised travel dates or applicable refunds based on the circumstances and supplier conditions.

14. Events Beyond Reasonable Control

Travel arrangements may be affected by circumstances outside the reasonable control of Roamora Travels or the traveller.

Such circumstances may include:

  • Severe weather or natural disasters
  • Floods, landslides or road closures
  • Government restrictions or official orders
  • Strikes or transportation disruption
  • Civil disturbance or security concerns
  • Public-health emergencies
  • Unexpected closure of destinations or attractions
  • Other events that materially affect travel operations

In such circumstances, refunds, credits, rescheduling and cancellation charges may depend on the policies of the individual travel service providers involved.

15. Weather-Related Changes

Weather conditions can affect mountain journeys, road travel, flights, sightseeing, outdoor activities and other travel experiences.

Where weather conditions require itinerary changes, we may assist with reasonable alternatives where available. A change caused by weather does not automatically make all booked services eligible for a refund.

16. Flight, Train & Transport Cancellations

Flight, railway and other transportation services are generally subject to the cancellation and refund policies of the relevant transport provider.

Any cancellation charges, refund amounts, credits or rescheduling options offered by such providers will normally be governed by their applicable fare rules and conditions.

17. Hotel & Accommodation Cancellations

Hotels and accommodation providers may apply their own cancellation deadlines and charges.

Some accommodation rates may permit cancellation within a specified period, while promotional, peak-season or special-rate bookings may be partially or completely non-refundable.

18. Peak Season & Special Periods

Travel during peak seasons, festivals, public holidays, special events and high-demand periods may be subject to stricter cancellation conditions.

Hotels and other suppliers may require larger advance payments or apply non-refundable booking conditions during these periods. Any special conditions applicable to your journey should be reviewed before confirming the booking.

19. Travel Insurance

Travellers may wish to consider suitable travel insurance that provides appropriate protection for their individual journey.

Depending on the policy selected, travel insurance may provide protection for certain unexpected cancellations, medical emergencies, delays, lost baggage or other covered events.

Insurance coverage, exclusions and claims are governed by the relevant insurance provider and policy terms.

20. Third-Party Service Providers

Many travel arrangements involve independent third-party providers, including hotels, airlines, transportation companies, guides and activity operators.

Cancellation and refund conditions imposed by these providers may apply in addition to the general terms described in this policy.

21. Payment & Transaction Charges

Where a payment provider, bank or other financial service has charged transaction or processing fees, such charges may not always be recoverable as part of a cancellation refund.

Any applicable deductions will depend on the payment method and the conditions associated with the relevant transaction.

22. Booking-Specific Cancellation Terms

Because every journey may include different hotels, routes, transportation and experiences, specific cancellation conditions may be provided with your quotation or booking confirmation.

Where booking-specific cancellation terms have been clearly communicated, those terms will apply to the relevant booking.

Please review before booking: Travellers should carefully review the quotation, itinerary, inclusions, payment requirements and cancellation conditions provided before confirming a journey.

23. Changes to This Cancellation Policy

Roamora Travels may update this Cancellation Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, booking procedures, supplier practices or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date where appropriate.

24. Related Policies

This Cancellation Policy should be read together with the policies and terms applicable to the Roamora Travels website and your booking.

  • Terms & Conditions
  • Privacy Policy

25. Contact Us

If you need to cancel or modify a travel arrangement, or if you have questions about the cancellation conditions applicable to your booking, please contact Roamora Travels as soon as possible.
