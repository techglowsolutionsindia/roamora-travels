✦ DESTINATION OVERVIEW

Where Mountains, Meet Timeless Beauty.

Kashmir brings together snow-covered mountains, peaceful lakes, green valleys and charming hill towns in one unforgettable Himalayan journey.

From relaxing shikara rides in Srinagar to scenic escapes in Gulmarg and Pahalgam, every part of Kashmir offers a different way to experience its natural beauty.

Ideal Stay 5–7 Days
Best For Nature & Couples
Travel Style Scenic & Relaxed
