Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how Roamora Travels collects, uses and protects the information you share while exploring or planning your journey with us.

Last Updated: October 2026

Roamora Travels respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide through our website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, why we collect it, how it may be used and the measures we take to safeguard it.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information when you contact us, request a travel quotation, submit a custom trip enquiry, subscribe to our newsletter or otherwise communicate with Roamora Travels.

This information may include:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Preferred travel destination
  • Travel dates or preferred travel period
  • Number of travellers
  • Travel preferences and interests
  • Budget or package preferences
  • Messages and other information submitted through our forms

2. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted to Roamora Travels may be used to understand your travel requirements and provide relevant assistance.

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to travel and general enquiries
  • Prepare personalised itineraries and travel quotations
  • Assist with custom trip planning
  • Communicate regarding requested travel services
  • Provide destination and package information
  • Improve our website, services and customer experience
  • Maintain appropriate enquiry and customer records
  • Send travel inspiration or updates when you have chosen to subscribe

3. Travel & Contact Enquiries

When you submit an enquiry through our website, the information entered in the form may be recorded so that our team can review your requirements and contact you regarding your requested journey or service.

Please provide only information that is reasonably necessary for us to understand and respond to your enquiry.

4. Newsletter Subscriptions

If you subscribe to the Roamora Travels newsletter, we may collect your email address together with basic subscription information such as the subscription date, time and source.

We may use this information to send destination inspiration, seasonal travel ideas, useful travel advice, travel stories and other Roamora Travels updates.

You may choose to stop receiving marketing communications from us at any time by using an available unsubscribe option or by contacting us.

5. Cookies & Website Information

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, understand how visitors interact with the website and improve browsing performance.

Depending on the services used by the website, certain technical information may also be processed automatically, such as browser type, device type, pages visited, referring pages and general website usage information.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not function as intended when certain cookies are disabled.

6. Third-Party Services

Roamora Travels may use trusted third-party services to operate and improve the website, process enquiries, maintain records, communicate with travellers or provide other supporting functions.

These services may process information only as necessary for the relevant function and may operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Sharing of Personal Information

Roamora Travels does not sell or rent your personal information.

Where necessary to respond to your request or arrange travel services, relevant information may be shared with appropriate service providers or business partners involved in fulfilling those services.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal requirements or protect the rights and security of Roamora Travels and its users.

8. Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, misuse, loss or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, while we take reasonable precautions, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, provide requested services, maintain appropriate business records, meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.

Information that is no longer reasonably required may be deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

10. Your Choices & Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may contact Roamora Travels if you would like to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information that you have provided to us.

You may also contact us if you wish to withdraw a communication preference or have questions regarding how your information is being handled.

11. External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, travel providers, social-media platforms or other external services. Roamora Travels is not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of websites operated by third parties.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website before submitting personal information.

12. Children's Privacy

Our website and travel enquiry services are intended for people who are legally able to make travel enquiries or for adults making travel arrangements on behalf of their families.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children without appropriate involvement from a parent or legal guardian.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Roamora Travels may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, business practices or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page and the “Last Updated” date may be revised accordingly.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the personal information you have provided to Roamora Travels, please contact us through our website.
